- (decision) Platform: Android first (author uses Android; iOS analog exists but no good Android analog found); other platforms later
- (decision) Stakes: launch to Google Play + portfolio piece for resume; author is first user
- (decision) v1 scope: NO camera/UGC photos; roadmap: user photos at locations with light moderation, venues layer (food/coffee/toilet/shelter), curated venue collections per district
- (decision) Working mode: coaching path, journey-led entry (consumer UX-heavy product)
- (decision) Competitive scan: Fog of World (paid 0, global travel scale, local-only data), MysteryHike (freemium, city gamification, EN-only, subscription), City Wanderer / Life in Mist / FOG map (RU-language analogs exist). WalkQuest wedge: free RU-first, city-scale on-foot focus, anti-cheat speed limits, walk+bike modes
- (decision) UJ-1 protagonist: Olya, 28, no walking habit; trigger = boredom + good weather; sets walk goal in app (distance/steps/% revealed); app runs in background with screen off; quiet non-intrusive notifications; end screen = praise + achievements + % revealed + time/distance + share button + 'come back tomorrow'
- (decision) Auto-pause after 15 min of user inactivity/stop; manual finish button
- (decision) Onboarding goal-setting at registration: walks per week OR daily time/distance; drives reminder notifications ('week ends, 2 walks left')
- (decision) Walk planning: schedule a walk (tomorrow 12:00), reminder notification; weather-aware notification hints (rain -> take umbrella) is LATER version
- (decision) Challenges/quests mechanic: random card OR pick 1 of 6; types: photo-N-purple-things, notice architecture, compliment a stranger, interval walking 3x
- (decision) NO in-app background music; users listen to their own music in headphones
- (decision) Challenges run on honor system, no proof: photos taken in native camera app OUTSIDE WalkQuest; user self-marks completion; challenge may suggest making a collage and sharing to social; in-app photo capture deferred to later version
- (decision) Account model: local-first storage of tracks on device; leaderboard uses nickname only (no tracks uploaded); MUST have data export/transfer option for device change; full cloud account rejected for privacy complexity
- (decision) Monetization: audience first, monetization later; v1 free
- (decision) Fog model: v1 tile grid (quad grid) fog reveal + city % counted over WALKABLE tiles only from OSM (roads, footpaths, park trails/eco-trails count); water/forest/industrial excluded; pedestrian-impossible motorways (e.g. SPb ZSD, KAD) explicitly excluded; v2+ adds street-completion layer over street network
- (decision) POI content: v1 auto-generated from OpenStreetMap + Wikipedia extracts; later updates rewrite descriptions manually or hybrid
- (decision) Leaderboard: two tabs - by city and by friends; metrics: distance walked and % map revealed
- (decision) Offline: walk records locally and fog reveals offline; leaderboard and POI sync when network returns
- (decision) Region model: user marks home city -> loads city + oblast (ecotrails count); traveling to another city adds that city+oblast; main map = current region; tab to browse previously visited regions
- (decision) Terminology: NOT 'fog of war', just fog/veil - no war associations; app name WalkQuest is working title, naming TBD
- (decision) Courtyards count as exploration zones: passing a block's roads does NOT unlock the whole block; inner passages count where accessible (paths in residential blocks, squares, forest parks)
- (decision) Autopause auto-resumes on movement (coffee stop scenario confirmed); if still no movement after another ~15 min, send reminder 'took a break or forgot to stop the walk?'
- (decision) Short onboarding tutorial explaining modes, to prevent 'why doesn't scooter count' frustration when wrong mode selected
- (decision) Challenges: optional, tied to walk, observation/mild interaction focus; no forced skip; 'add challenge' button, hideable in settings for users who never want them
- (decision) POI confirmed as beacons visible through fog
- (decision) Battery guard: at 15% device battery notify 'walk will auto-finish at 10% so you can get home comfortably'
- (decision) Design direction: modern, not childish, no big bright 3D elements, not beige/monotone, NO medieval styling (a competitor has it); dark + light theme; palette customization in later versions
- (decision) OQ-7 downgraded: OSM coverage in SPb + LO confirmed good by author's local knowledge (yard paths, parks present; some zakaznik trails partially missing - minor; popular routes detailed). Yandex/2GIS data confirmed legally unusable (no storage/extraction allowed)
- (decision) OQ-5 resolved: nickname requested at first leaderboard open, not at onboarding; review findings folded in (NFR thresholds deferred to architecture doc, leaderboard sync cadence, 6h walk + process-death restore, city=Region tab note)
- (event) PRD finalized
